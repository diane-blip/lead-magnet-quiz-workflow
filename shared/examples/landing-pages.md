Above-Fold Asset Stack:

- Eyebrow + Headline + Subheadline + Trust signals + Form visible in 5 seconds, no scroll.
- Test on iPhone Safari. If it doesn't fit, you lose 60% of traffic.
1. Value Stack:
- Show $1,000+ value for $0 investment. Break into 3-5 components with realistic dollar values.
- Make perceived value 10-20x asking price. Saying no should feel stupid.
1. Mobile-First Squeeze:
- Design for iPhone first, then enhance for desktop. Compress hero section aggressively.
- 60%+ of traffic is mobile. Form above fold or conversion dies.
1. Specific Numbers Always:
- "$3M+ generated" not "made money". "2,847 members" not "thousands".
- Boring beats shiny. Specific beats vague. Numbers create believability.
1. Real Testimonials/Facts Only:
- 3 actual quotes from real people with full names + roles/companies.
- Focus on specific outcomes. Fake testimonials destroy trust forever.
1. Research First, Always:
- Audit existing emails, posts, testimonials before writing anything.
- Pull real numbers from actual data. Can't build on a fake foundation.
1. Contrarian Positioning:
- "While everyone's chasing [popular thing]" sets you apart immediately.
- Position as operator who built it, not guru who discovered secrets.
1. Problem-Agitate-Solve:
- List 3 pain points → Amplify the cost → "Look, I spent [time] building this."
- Emotional connection before rational value stack. Pain moves faster than gain.
1. A/B Test Headlines First:
- Headline is 80% of conversion. Test 5+ variations before touching anything else.
- Track everything from day one. Can't optimize what you don't measure.
