For segments Use **one skeleton** and only swap a few words in subject line/opening sentence based on segment

## Performance Benchmarks

**Welcome sequences:** 4x more opens and 5x more clicks vs regular emails

**Hot leads:** 60-80% conversion in 7-21 days

**Warm leads:** 10-25% conversion in 30-60 days

**Cold leads:** 1-5% conversion over 90-180 days

**Soft CTAs:** 42% higher reply rates than hard asks in early outreach

## Sequence 1: Welcome Sequence (Hot Leads)

**Best for:** Lead magnet opt-ins, webinar signups, discovery call bookings

**Length:** 7 emails over 14 days

**Expected conversion:** 15-25% to meeting

| Day | Subject | Focus | CTA | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | "Your [Lead Magnet] is Here + What's Next" | Deliver promise immediately, set expectations, personal story, ask for reply with their challenge | Click to Access | Strike while iron hot |
| 1 | "The Mistake That Keeps [Niche] Stuck" | #1 obstacle, why traditional fails, ONE actionable tip | Try This Today | Maintain momentum |
| 3 | "How [Client] Went From $X to $Y in [Time]" | Detailed transformation with numbers, timeline, obstacles, process focus | See Full Story | Social proof + specifics = credibility |
| 5 | "The Truth About [Common False Belief]" | Challenge accepted belief, contrarian perspective, client proof | Learn My Methodology | Myth-busting gets high engagement |
| 7 | "Why I Almost Quit Coaching (And Why I'm Glad I Didn't)" | Vulnerable personal moment, lesson learned, how it helps clients | Learn How This Helps | Vulnerability builds trust |
| 10 | "3 Signs You're Ready (And 2 You're Not)" | Self-assessment framework, cost of waiting, objection handling | Are You Ready? | Self-qualification + urgency |
| 14 | "Ready to See What's Possible? Let's Talk" | Confident offer with results, address objections, limited availability | Book Discovery Call | Direct soft sell after warmup |

**Customization by type:**

- **Life Coaches:** Transformation stories, mindset shifts, freedom outcomes
- **Business Coaches:** ROI, revenue, systems, measurable growth
- **Career Coaches:** Salary increases, promotions, job satisfaction

**Pro tip:** Send Day 0 email within seconds of signup—timing matters more than perfect copy.

## Sequence 2: Cold Lead Nurture

**Best for:** Cold audience, lead magnet lists, bottom-of-funnel prospects

**Length:** 6-10 emails over 3-4 weeks, every 2-3 days

**Expected conversion:** 5-12% engagement, 1-3% meetings

| # | Subject Pattern | Focus | CTA | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "[#] [Niche] Are [Problem]—But It Doesn't Have to Be" | Identify problem, validate frustration, position as different | Does this sound familiar? | Day 0 (soft intro) |
| 2 | "[Client] Made This Mistake (Cost Them [Impact])" | Case study: before → what went wrong → lesson, quantify cost | See what changed | Day 3 |
| 3 | "The Myth About [False Belief] (Here's What Works)" | Contrarian take backed by experience, surprising insight | Read my approach | Day 5 |
| 4 | "The One Thing Most [Niche] Miss" | Educational insight—teach something useful regardless of buy | Download framework | Day 7 |
| 5 | "[Client] Almost Quit (Before This One Shift)" | Vulnerability + persistence, methodology connection, transformation | How did they overcome? | Day 10 |
| 6 | "Why [Traditional Method] Doesn't Work Anymore" | Compare approaches, explain why old fails, position as ahead | See the better way | Day 12 |
| 7 | "The Hidden Cost of [Problem]" | Quantify impact (revenue, time, opportunities), light urgency | Calculate your cost | Day 15 |
| 8 | "Are You Ready for Change?" | Readiness assessment, address top objections, soft close | Is this the right timing? | Day 18 |
| 9 | "[Service] Investment Starts at $X (What's Included)" | Pricing in value context, payment plans, limited spots/deadline | Book time to discuss | Day 21 |
| 10 | "Final Chance to Join [Cohort]—Closes Friday" | Scarcity, recap transformation, testimonials, specific deadline | Secure your spot | Day 24 |

**Cadence strategy:**

- If opens drop below 15%, space emails 4 days apart instead of 3
- If opens stay above 25%, maintain 2-3 day spacing

**CTA strategy:**

- Avoid calendar links in early emails (1-6)
- Use soft questions: "Does this sound relevant?" "Worth discussing?"
- Questions generate 42% higher reply rates vs "Let's schedule"
- Save calendar/booking link for email #9+ after warmup

## Sequence 3: Warm Lead Activation

**Best for:** Subscribers who clicked 2+ emails, watched video, visited pricing

**Length:** 4-6 emails over 8-10 days

**Expected conversion:** 15-30% to call booking

| # | Subject | Focus | CTA | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "[Name] - I Noticed You Checking Something Out" | Acknowledge specific action, show attention, offer value | Want quick explanation? | Same day |
| 2 | "[Client Type] Like You Chose [Service]—Here's Why" | Relevant case matching profile, specific ROI, timeline | Can you see yourself in this? | Day 2 |
| 3 | "The #1 Question I Get About [Service]" | Address likely hesitation, targeted testimonial, reassurance | Want to explore if right fit? | Day 4 |
| 4 | "[Service] Options Broken Down (Simple Version)" | Clear comparison of 2-3 tiers, pros/cons, help self-select | Which makes most sense? | Day 6 |
| 5 | "One More Thing About [Guarantee/Bonus]" | Address secondary objection or add incentive, soft close | Ready for next step? | Day 8 |

**CTA strategy:**

- Use soft-to-medium CTAs (not aggressive booking demands)
- Offer value first: "Want me to send case study?" vs "Schedule call"
- Can include calendar link in signature (not required in body)
- Positive reply rate: 68% vs 41% for hard CTAs

## Sequence 4: Hot Lead Conversion (Post-Discovery)

**Best for:** Recent discovery call attendees, high-engagement, asked for pricing

**Length:** 3-4 emails over 8 days

**Expected conversion:** 60-80% close rate

| # | Subject | Focus | CTA | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "[Name] - Your Custom Action Plan Inside" | Recap challenges from call, 2-3 immediate steps, personalized doc | Got questions? Reply | Within 2 hours |
| 2 | "[Client Like You] Had Same Concern You Mentioned" | Address exact objection from call, relevant success story | See how they overcame | Day 2 |
| 3 | "The Cost of Waiting (Someone Who Almost Missed Out)" | Story about delaying, quantify losses, investment options, deadline | Are you ready? | Day 5 |
| 4 | "[Name], Final Thoughts + Limited Spots Remaining" | Recap transformation, address objections, payment options, specific deadline | Claim Your Spot | Day 8 |

**CTA strategy:**

- Direct action language: "Schedule Now," "Claim Spot," "Start Journey" (2-4 words)
- Prominent button format—buttons outperform text by 45%
- Button size: minimum 44x44 pixels (mobile critical)
- Use calendar link tool (Calendly integrated)
- Specific time beats vague: "Friday 2pm" > "sometime this week"
- Meeting booking rate: 37% when done right

## Sequence 5: Breakup/Win-Back (Dormant)

**Best for:** No opens 60+ days, past customers who didn't renew

**Length:** 4 emails over 7-10 days

**Expected conversion:** 5-15% re-engagement, 20-30% unsubscribe

| # | Subject | Focus | CTA | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "Should we break up?" | Acknowledge silence, ask if email working, light humor, direct reply | Should I keep sending? | Day 0 |
| 2 | "What You Missed (Best Hits From [Time])" | Top 3 wins, popular content, breakthroughs, why it matters | Catch up here | Day 2 |
| 3 | "[Exclusive Offer] Just for You (48-Hour Window)" | "We miss you" angle, limited offer (discount, bonus, access) | Claim Comeback Offer | Day 5 |
| 4 | "Last Chance—Here's Your Options" | Explicit choice: stay, unsubscribe, or reply with feedback (no guilt) | Stay / Unsubscribe / Reply | Day 7 |

**Why include unsubscribe?**

- Increases trust, reduces spam complaints
- Healthier small engaged list > bloated unengaged list
- Compliant with regulations (GDPR, CAN-SPAM)
- 20-30% unsubscribe rate is healthy segmentation

## Subject Line Patterns by Temperature

### Cold Leads (Curiosity + Pain Point)

**Specific outcome:** "Helped 20+ [Niche] Double [Metric] in [Time]"

Example: "Helped 15 coaches book 12+ clients in 90 days"

**Myth-busting:** "The [Emotional] Truth About [False Belief]"

Example: "The Uncomfortable Truth About 'Follow Your Passion'"

**Short + specific:** 4-7 words, pain-specific (not generic)

Example: "Social media schedule" beats "Marketing tips"

**Curiosity + goal:** "[#] Ways to [Goal] Without [Pain]"

Example: "3 ways to book calls without being pushy"

### Warm Leads (Proof + Specificity)

**Personalized case:** "[Name] Went From [Before] to [After]—Here's How"

Example: "Sarah went from 2 clients to 12—here's the exact method"

**Relevance check:** "Wanted to Send This Your Way, [Name]"

Example: "Wanted to share this for you, David"

**Value asset:** "[Quick Resource] for [Their Challenge]"

Example: "5-minute framework for cold call scripts"

### Hot Leads (Urgency + Specificity)

**Scarcity:** "[Limited] Spots Left—Closes [Date]"

Example: "2 spots left in next cohort—closes Friday"

**Direct + specific:** "[Name], Your [Next Step] Is Ready"

Example: "Sarah, your onboarding calendar is ready"

**Time-specific:** "[Action] Today—[Incentive/Deadline]"

Example: "Confirm today—extra bonus if you commit this week"

### What Gets Opened

- **Personalized with name:** +20% open rate, +31% sales leads, -17% unsubscribes
- **Under 30 characters:** Best on mobile (60% of opens)
- **Goal-focused (not product):** ~22% higher open rate
- **Contains specific number/timeframe:** Curiosity trigger

## CTA Strategies by Temperature

### Cold Leads: Soft CTAs (Question Format)

Use questions for cold—generate **12% total replies with 68% positive** vs only 41% positive for direct asks.

**Working examples:**

- "Does this sound relevant?"
- "Worth a brief chat?"
- "Open to checking this out?"
- "Curious how you're solving this?"
- "Would this be useful for your team?"
- "Think this could apply to your situation?"

**Why soft works:** Respects autonomy, doesn't demand calendar commitment, reduces loss aversion fear.

**Delivery:** Plain text—no button, no calendar link in body. Just the question.

---

### Warm Leads: Soft-to-Medium CTAs (Permission-Based)

Lead with value offer, then soft ask.

**Working examples:**

- "Want me to send over that case study from [similar company]?"
- "Can I share how [Client] handles [their problem]?"
- "Open to a 15-minute exploration call if we're a fit?"
- "Would you find a quick breakdown of options helpful?"

**Delivery:** Can be button or text. Optional calendar link in signature, not required in body. Positive reply rate: 68%.

---

### Hot Leads: Hard CTAs (Direct, Urgent)

Once they've shown readiness (booked call, asked pricing, high engagement), direct CTAs work—**37% meeting booking rate**.

**Working examples:**

- "Book your discovery call here"
- "Claim your spot—2 left in this cohort"
- "Schedule Friday at 2pm" (specific beats vague)
- "Ready to get started? Secure your spot →"

**Delivery requirements:**

- **Button format** (not text link): +45% click-through
- **Button size:** Minimum 44x44 pixels (mobile)
- **Text length:** 2-4 words max ("Schedule Now" vs "Click here to schedule")
- **Use calendar tool:** Calendly, Acuity, etc.
- **Specific time wins:** "Friday 2pm" > "sometime this week"

## Mobile-First Design (60% Opens on Mobile)

1. **Subject line:** <30 characters (doesn't get cut off)
2. **Preview text:** Front-load value prop—preview should make them want to open
3. **Single column layout:** Stacked text, not side-by-side
4. **White space:** Plenty of breathing room, paragraphs 2-3 lines max
5. **Font size:** 16px minimum (readable without pinching)
6. **CTA buttons:** 44x44 pixels minimum (thumb-friendly), high contrast
7. **Links:** Every link tappable without zooming

**Test template:** Test on iPhone SE, Samsung Galaxy, iPad before sending.

## Segmentation & Automation Setup

### Lead Temperature Tagging

1. **Cold:** Just landed, no opens in first 7 days
2. **Warm:** 2+ opens, 1+ click, viewed content but not pricing/booking
3. **Hot:** Clicked pricing, completed form, booked call, 3+ emails opened

### Automation Rules

- **Cold leads** → 10-email nurture (2-3 days apart)
- **Warm leads** → 5-email activation (2-3 days apart)
- **Hot leads** → 3-4 email close (1-2 days apart, prominent calendar)
- **Dormant** (no opens 60 days) → 4-email win-back (expect 20-30% unsubscribe)

### CTA Variation by Temperature

- **Cold emails:** Plain text soft CTA questions
- **Warm emails:** Medium CTA (permission asks, optional button)
- **Hot emails:** Prominent button CTA with calendar link

## Key Metrics to Track

**Open Rate Targets:**

- Cold: 20-25%
- Warm: 30-40%
- Hot: 40-50%

**Click-Through Rate:**

- Cold: 2-4%
- Warm: 5-8%
- Hot: 8-15%

**Reply Rate:**

- Cold: 8-12%
- Warm: 15-20%
- Hot: 25-40%

**Booking Rate:**

- Cold: 0.5-1%
- Warm: 2-5%
- Hot: 30-50%

### If Metrics Lag, Adjust:

- Subject lines (test 5 variations)
- Email spacing (increase if opens drop below 15%)
- CTA copy (soft vs hard)
- Send time (test mornings vs afternoons)
