# Voice Non-Negotiables (SRC)

Every funnel this workflow builds is delivered by Sway Rise Creative. The **copy register matches the client's own brand voice** (detected from their site by the research/design agents) — that part does not change per client. But the rules below are universal: they apply on top of any client's voice, on every piece of copy (landing page, quiz, emails, loading messages, strategy docs, preview pages).

The copy agent and any agent that writes client-facing text must apply these. They are pass/fail.

## Hard bans

- **No em dashes.** Use a period, comma, parentheses, or rewrite.
- **No negative parallelism.** Ban "not just X, but Y" and "it's not X, it's Y."
- **No AI-tell vocabulary:** delve, leverage (verb), unlock, supercharge, unleash, harness, navigate (the landscape of), realm, journey (metaphorical), cutting-edge, game-changer, revolutionary, paradigm shift, robust, seamless, elevate, empower, foster, embark, enhance, "in today's fast-paced world."
- **No hype rhythm.** No three-beat triples for emphasis.
- **Never name the host.** Cloudflare, Workers, D1, Astro, or "the host/hosting provider" must never appear in client-facing copy. Say "your site," "hosting," "looked after," "taken care of." (Kit may be named — the client owns their Kit account. WordPress/Squarespace may be named only when contrasting with what a client is leaving behind.)
- **Never "handled."** Use "looked after," "running smoothly," "taken care of."

## Always

- **Concrete over abstract.** Specific outcomes, specific numbers, specific moments.
- **Earn every claim.** No unsupported superlatives.
- **Second person.** The quiz taker / reader is the hero. The client's business is the guide.

## Scope note

These are SRC's *universal* rules, not SRC's full brand voice. Do **not** impose SRC's own register (its pillars, its taglines) onto a client's funnel — the funnel speaks in the client's voice. These rules are the floor under that voice. Full reference lives in the vault: `CLAUDE.md` (Universal non-negotiables) and `wiki/voice/`.
