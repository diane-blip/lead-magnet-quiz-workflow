Backend Infrastructure

Lead capture wired to your email account, with analytics built in

Where Your Leads Live

Every lead lands in your own email account, not in a system we control. When someone finishes the quiz, their contact details, quiz score, and segment (hot, warm, or cold) flow straight into your list, tagged and ready for the right email sequence.

  • You own the list:Leads go to your email account, so the relationship is yours
  • Tagged automatically:Each lead is sorted by score and profile on the way in
  • Routed to the right sequence:Hot, warm, and cold each start the matching emails

How Submissions Are Processed

  • Quiz submit:Scores the quiz, personalizes the result, and adds the lead to your email account with the right tags
  • Event logging:Records funnel events (page views, clicks, completions) for your dashboard
  • Dashboard data:Serves your analytics, password protected

Analytics Storage

The only data we store is anonymous funnel analytics, kept in a single lightweight table so your dashboard stays fast. No lead data is held outside your own email account.
