Questions & Answers

How long does it take?

10 days from kickoff to live funnel. This isn't a rushed template job—it's a thorough process that includes market research, custom copy, and a fully built system.

What do I need to provide?

Access to your website/products and a kickoff call. We handle the research, strategy, copy, and build. You approve.

Can I customize the output?

Absolutely. You own all source files. The quiz, landing page, and email sequences are yours to modify and deploy however you like.

How does the automated nurturing work?

Leads are automatically scored based on their quiz responses and routed into email sequences matched to their readiness level. Hot leads get direct CTAs. Cold leads get educational content.

What platforms does this work with?

The quiz deploys anywhere (it's a standalone web app). Email sequences export to any platform—Mailchimp, ConvertKit, ActiveCampaign, you name it.
