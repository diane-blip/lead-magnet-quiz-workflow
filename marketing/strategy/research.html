Executive Summary

The quiz funnel market is fragmented between DIY tools ($27-600/mo) and full-service agencies ($5K-15K). Both alternatives have significant gaps:

  • DIY Tools (Interact, Outgrow, Typeform): Provide quiz builders but no email automation, no analytics, no deployment, generic templates
  • Agencies: Expensive, slow (80+ hours), opaque process, limited revisions
  • Market Opportunity: Complete system that's custom (not template), fast (not months), and includes email automation + analytics + deployment

Key Insight: Buyers searching "interactive quiz" (2400/mo) far outnumber those searching "quiz funnel" (260/mo). Most don't know they need a complete funnel—they think they need a quiz tool.

Customer Pain Points (From Reviews & Forums)

Pain Point 1: Generic Templates

"Interact templates look like everyone else's quiz"

  • Tools provide templates, but customization is limited
  • Buyers want something that matches their brand
  • Templates signal "cheap" or "unprofessional"

Pain Point 2: Incomplete Solution

"Got the quiz working but now I need a developer for the website, a copywriter for emails, and Zapier to connect everything"

  • Quiz tools only solve 20% of the problem
  • Still need: designer, copywriter, developer, email tool, analytics
  • Hidden costs add up quickly

Pain Point 3: Expensive at Scale

"Interact is $125/mo for 96K leads, but I'm already at 50K and need to upgrade"

  • Usage-based pricing punishes growth
  • Buyers feel trapped by scaling costs
  • Considering alternatives but migration is painful

Pain Point 4: No Automation

"I have to manually tag and segment leads after the quiz"

  • Tools capture emails but don't send sequences
  • Manual work required for segmentation
  • No automated nurture based on quiz results

Pain Point 5: Agency Risk

"Paid $8K upfront and after 3 weeks got a design I hated, revisions were $1,200 more"

  • High upfront cost with uncertain outcome
  • Opaque process (can't see work in progress)
  • Revisions cost extra
  • No refunds or guarantees
