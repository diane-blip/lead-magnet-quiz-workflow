Production Tips

Filming Best Practices

  • Film vertically (9:16 aspect ratio)
  • Good lighting on your face
  • Clean background or relevant setting
  • Maintain eye contact with camera
  • Energy in the first 3 seconds is crucial
  • Use captions (most watch muted)

Posting Strategy

  • Post 1-2 per day during testing
  • Best times: 7-9am, 12-2pm, 7-9pm
  • Use 3-5 relevant hashtags
  • Pin top performer to profile

CTA Variations

  • "Comment [KEYWORD]" - drives engagement
  • "Save this" - signals value to algorithm
  • "Follow for more" - grows audience
  • "Link in bio" - drives traffic (use sparingly)
