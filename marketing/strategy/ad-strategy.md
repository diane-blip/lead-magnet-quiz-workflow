## Executive Summary

### The Opportunity

Coaches and course creators spend $2,000-10,000/month on Instagram ads driving traffic to basic landing pages. Their conversion rates are typically 1-3%. Quiz funnels convert at 5-15%—a 3-5x improvement.

**Your positioning:** "I can make your existing ad spend work 3x harder."

### Key Strategy Points

- **Format:** Reels-first (50%+ of Instagram ads now run on Reels)
- **Objective:** Messages or Leads (not Traffic)
- **Offer:** Free funnel audit → Discovery call → $3-5k service
- **Budget:** $15-20/day testing, scale winners to $50+/day

### Why This Works

1. **You're selling to people already spending money** — they understand ROI
2. **Reels have lowest CPM** (~$3-4 vs $6-8 for feed) — cheaper reach
3. **Quiz funnels are perfect for coaches** — they already believe in segmentation
4. **Your 7-day delivery is a pattern interrupt** — agencies take 6 weeks

## Platform & Format Strategy

### Why Reels First

According to CNBC and Sensor Tower data (January 2026):
- **50%+ of all Instagram ads now run on Reels** (up from 35% in 2024)
- **46% of time spent on Instagram is on Reels** (up from 37% in 2024)
- **Reels CPM is $3-4** vs Feed CPM of $6-8

**Reels outperform because:**
- Vertical, full-screen = higher engagement
- Algorithm pushes Reels content
- Feels native/organic, less "ad-like"
- 55% of Reel views come from non-followers (discovery)

### Format Priority

| Format | Use For | CPM | CTR |
|--------|---------|-----|-----|
| **Reels** (Primary) | Cold traffic, awareness, education | $3-4 | 0.30-0.50% |
| **Stories** (Secondary) | Retargeting, urgency offers | $5-6 | 0.33-0.54% |
| **Feed** (Tertiary) | Carousel proof, testimonials | $6-8 | 0.78% |

### Video Best Practices (From Research)

- **Length:** Under 15 seconds performs best
- **Hook:** First 2 seconds must grab attention
- **Sound:** Sound-on Reels get higher completion
- **Style:** Raw/authentic beats polished
- **Format:** Vertical (9:16) only
- **Text:** Captions essential (most watch muted first)

## Ad Creative Concepts

### Concept 1: "The Funnel Roast" (Screen Recording)

**Format:** 30-60 second Reel
**Style:** Screen recording with voiceover
**Vibe:** Educational, slightly provocative

**What you do:**
1. Screen record yourself going through a coach's funnel (after clicking their ad)
2. Point out what's wrong (generic landing page, no personalization, etc.)
3. Show how a quiz version would improve it
4. CTA to free audit

**Why it works:**
- Pattern interrupt (not typical ad)
- Demonstrates expertise without claiming it
- Specific = credible
- Creates "I wonder what my funnel looks like" curiosity

**Script outline:**
```
[Screen recording of generic funnel]
"I clicked on a coach's ad and their funnel made me cringe..."
[Point at generic landing page]
"See this? Basic opt-in page. No personalization. No segmentation."
[Show what they're missing]
"They're paying $5-10 per lead and have no idea which ones are actually ready to buy."
[Quick visual of quiz alternative]
"A quiz funnel would score these leads automatically and send personalized emails based on their answers."
[Face to camera]
"Want me to roast YOUR funnel? Free audit, link in bio."
```

| Creative Testing | Week 1-2 | $15-20/day | $210-280 |

| **Total** | 4 weeks | - | **$560-805** |

### Week 1-2: Creative Testing

**Objective:** Find which creative concept resonates

**Setup:**
- 1 Campaign, Messages objective
- 1 Ad Set, Audience A (Platform Users)
- 4 Ads (one for each creative concept)
- Budget: $15-20/day split across ads

**Success metrics:**
- CTR > 0.5% = keep testing
- CTR < 0.3% after 1,000 impressions = kill
- Cost per message < $10 = potential winner

**Rules:**
- Don't touch for 72 hours
- Kill clear losers after 3-4 days
- Double down on winners

### Week 3: Audience Testing

**Objective:** Find which audience converts best

**Setup:**
- Take winning creative(s) from Week 1-2
- Create 3 Ad Sets (Audiences A, B, C)
- Same ads in each
- Budget: $20-25/day split across ad sets

**Success metrics:**
- Cost per message comparison
- Message quality (are they qualified?)
- Show rate if booking calls

### Week 4: Scale Winners

**Objective:** Maximize profitable reach

**Setup:**
- Best creative + best audience
- Increase budget 20% every 2-3 days
- Add retargeting campaign ($5-10/day)

**Scaling rules:**
- Only scale if profitable for 5+ days
- Never more than 20% increase at once
- If performance drops, pause and diagnose

## Creative Refresh Schedule

**Frequency:** Every 2-3 weeks or when performance drops 20%+

**Refresh options:**
1. New hook, same concept
2. New visual, same script
3. New format (carousel instead of video)
4. Testimonial/case study version
5. Seasonal angle

**Signs of fatigue:**
- CTR drops 20%+ from baseline
- Frequency > 3 (same person seeing ad 3+ times)
- Comments like "I keep seeing this ad"

## Quick Reference: What to Do If...

**CTR is below 0.3%:**
- Hook isn't working → Test new opening
- Creative is boring → Add movement, pattern interrupt
- Targeting too broad → Add interest suggestions

**Getting clicks but no messages:**
- Landing page friction → Simplify form
- Offer not clear → Clarify what they get
- Wrong objective → Switch to Messages

**Messages but no calls booked:**
- Response time too slow → Set up auto-reply
- Follow-up weak → Improve email sequence
- Qualification issue → Add screening questions

**Calls but no sales:**
- Wrong leads → Adjust targeting or creative
- Sales process issue → Not an ads problem
- Price objection → Address in retargeting content
