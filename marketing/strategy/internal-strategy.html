Executive Summary

Core Positioning: The only quiz funnel service that delivers a complete, production-ready system (landing page + quiz + email automation + analytics + deployment) in 7 days—not months, not puzzle pieces.

Market Opportunity

The quiz funnel market is fragmented between DIY tools ($27-600/mo) and full-service agencies ($5K-15K). Both have critical gaps:

20%
What DIY tools actually deliver
3-6 weeks
Agency timeline (vs. our 15 min)

Key Strategic Insight

Buyers searching "interactive quiz" (2,400/mo) outnumber those searching "quiz funnel" (260/mo) by 9:1. Most don't know they need a complete funnel—they think they need a quiz tool.

Implication: Our marketing must educate before selling. Show them the gap between "quiz tool" and "complete system."

Recommended Primary Angle

Angle #3: The Hidden Cost — Position against DIY tools by exposing the hidden work required. Resonate with buyers comparing Interact/Outgrow who don't realize they're only getting 20% of the solution.

Pain Point Matrix

Pain Point #1: Generic Templates

"Interact templates look like everyone else's quiz"

Emotional Pain: Brand looks cheap/unprofessional

Rational Pain: Lower conversion rates, no differentiation

Urgency: Live with it or pay designer $500-2,000 for custom design

Our Solution: Every quiz custom-researched and brand-matched in 7 days

Pain Point #2: Incomplete Solution (The Hidden 80%)

"Got the quiz working but now I need a developer for the website, a copywriter for emails, and Zapier to connect everything"

Emotional Pain: Frustration, feeling deceived ("I thought this was the complete solution")

Rational Pain: $2,500-7,800 additional costs + $130-500/mo ongoing

Hidden costs breakdown: - Designer for landing page: $500-2,000 - Copywriter for email sequences: $500-1,500 - Developer for analytics: $1,000-3,000 - Email tool (Mailchimp, ConvertKit): $50-200/mo - Analytics tool: $50-200/mo - Zapier/integrations: $30-100/mo TOTAL: $2,500-7,800 + $130-500/mo

Urgency: Can't launch until these are built (weeks/months delay)

Our Solution: Complete system in 7 days (no hidden costs)

Pain Point #3: Expensive at Scale

"Interact is $125/mo for 96K leads, but I'm already at 50K and need to upgrade"

Emotional Pain: Feeling trapped, penalized for success

Rational Pain: Usage-based pricing increases with growth

Urgency: Hit limit, forced to upgrade or stop capturing leads

Our Solution: One-time cost, unlimited leads

Pain Point #4: No Automation

"I have to manually tag and segment leads after the quiz"

Emotional Pain: Overwhelm, time drain

Rational Pain: Manual work doesn't scale, leads fall through cracks

Urgency: Daily/weekly manual work or hire VA ($15-30/hr)

Our Solution: Automatic email sequences based on quiz temperature

Pain Point #5: Agency Risk

"Paid $8K upfront and after 3 weeks got a design I hated, revisions were $1,200 more"

Emotional Pain: Regret, betrayal, financial stress

Rational Pain: $5K-15K sunk cost, no guarantee, 3-6 week delay

Urgency: Need to launch, pressure to accept subpar work

Our Solution: 15-minute delivery, regenerate if not happy (no revision fees)

Objection Handling

Objection: "Can I just use Typeform?"

Response: "Typeform is a form builder. It doesn't score leads, segment by temperature, or send automated email sequences. You'd still need to build the landing page, write the emails, set up the automation, and create the analytics. We give you the complete system."

Objection: "Why not hire an agency?"

Response: "Agencies charge $5K-15K and take 3-6 weeks. Even then, they don't include email automation or analytics. You'll pay $1,200+ for revisions. We deliver in 7 days with everything included, and if you need changes, regenerate in 7 days."

Objection: "Interact is only $27/mo"

Response: "Interact gives you the quiz interface. You still need to: - Design the landing page ($500-2,000) - Write the quiz copy ($300-800) - Write 14 email sequences ($500-1,500) - Set up email automation ($80-300/mo) - Build an analytics dashboard ($1,000-3,000) - Deploy everything ($500-1,000) Total hidden cost: $2,880-8,600 + $80-300/mo. We include all of that."

Objection: "This sounds too automated/generic"

Response: "Every quiz is custom-researched for your market. We analyze your website, competitors, products, and audience. The design matches your brand. The copy uses your voice. The questions target your specific customers. It's not a template—it's custom-built in 7 days using AI agents."

Objection: "Can I customize it after?"

Response: "Yes. All code is yours, fully editable, and deployed to your own site. Want to change copy? Edit the HTML. Want different questions? Regenerate in 7 days with new inputs. You own everything."

Objection: "What if I need changes/revisions?"

Response: "Just regenerate. Unlike agencies that charge $1,200 for revisions and take another week, you can regenerate your entire quiz funnel in 7 days. Try different angles, different products, different copy—as many times as you want."

Objection: "Does it work for my industry?"

Response: "We've built quiz funnels for: catering, plumbing, coaching, SaaS, e-commerce, consulting, agencies. The workflow researches your specific market and adapts. Show me your website and I'll walk you through how it would work for you."

Objection: "I need to think about it"

Response: "Fair enough. What questions can I answer to help you decide? [pause] Most people are comparing us to Interact or considering an agency. Which route were you leaning toward?" [Uncover real objection, then address it]

Sales Enablement

Discovery Questions

  1. "What would you use this quiz for?" → Uncover use case (lead gen, product recommendation, segmentation)
  2. "Have you tried quiz tools like Interact or Outgrow?" → Identify pain points with DIY tools
  3. "What stopped you from hiring an agency?" → Surface price/time objection
  4. "How are you currently capturing and nurturing leads?" → Establish baseline (often manual or nonexistent)
  5. "How quickly do you need this launched?" → Speed advantage (can deploy today)
  6. "What's your monthly lead volume?" → Expose Interact's scaling costs
  7. "Who would manage this day-to-day?" → Emphasize automation (no manual work)

Price: $2,500

Positioning: Middle of agency range, 7-day delivery, complete system

Value prop: "Get what an agency charges $5K-15K for, delivered in 7 days"

Pros: Clear value comparison, no recurring objection, feels like a deal

Option 2: Monthly SaaS

Price: $147-297/mo

Positioning: Complete system vs. incomplete DIY tools

Value prop: "Same price as Interact, but you get the complete system"

Pros: Recurring revenue, lower barrier

Cons: Competes with "cheap" tools, need to justify monthly fee

Option 3: Per-Quiz

Price: $497-997 per quiz

Positioning: Usage-based, scale at your own pace

Value prop: "Only pay when you need a new quiz"

Pros: Low initial commitment, upsell potential

Option 4: Hybrid

Price: $497 one-time + $47/mo for updates

Positioning: Low barrier + recurring value

Value prop: "Get started for $497, keep it updated for $47/mo"

Pros: Best of both worlds

Recommended Pricing Strategy

Start with Option 1: $2,500 one-time
  • Clear value comparison to agencies ($5K-15K)
  • No recurring objection (vs. SaaS pricing)
  • Feels like a deal ("agency quality, half the price")
  • Simple positioning (complete system, 7-day delivery, one price)
Test other options if:
  • $2,500 is too high a barrier (try $1,497 entry point)
  • Want recurring revenue (try $147-297/mo SaaS)
  • Targeting agencies (try $997/quiz + white label)
