Platform & Format Strategy

Key Data Point: According to CNBC and Sensor Tower (January 2026), 50%+ of all Instagram ads now run on Reels, up from 35% in 2024. Reels CPM is $3-4 vs Feed CPM of $6-8.

Why Reels First

  • 46% of time spent on Instagram is on Reels (up from 37% in 2024)
  • Vertical, full-screen = higher engagement
  • Algorithm pushes Reels content
  • 55% of Reel views come from non-followers (discovery)

Format Priority

Format Use For CPM CTR
Reels (Primary) Cold traffic, awareness, education $3-4 0.30-0.50%
Stories (Secondary) Retargeting, urgency offers $5-6 0.33-0.54%
Feed (Tertiary) Carousel proof, testimonials $6-8 0.78%

Video Best Practices

Do

  • Keep under 15 seconds
  • Hook in first 2 seconds
  • Use sound (higher completion)
  • Raw/authentic style
  • Vertical (9:16) only
  • Add captions (most watch muted)

Don't

  • Over-produce (feels like an ad)
  • Start with logo
  • Use stock footage
  • Make horizontal videos
  • Bake text into video
  • Go over 60 seconds

Ad Creative Concepts

CONCEPT 1

The Funnel Roast (Screen Recording)

Format: 30-60 second Reel | Style: Screen recording with voiceover | Vibe: Educational, slightly provocative

What You Do

  1. Screen record yourself going through a coach's funnel (after clicking their ad)
  2. Point out what's wrong (generic landing page, no personalization)
  3. Show how a quiz version would improve it
  4. CTA to free audit

Script

[Screen recording of generic funnel]

"I clicked on a coach's ad and their funnel made me cringe..."

[Point at generic landing page]

"See this? Basic opt-in page. No personalization. No segmentation."

[Show what they're missing]

"They're paying $5-10 per lead and have no idea which ones are actually ready to buy."

[Quick visual of quiz alternative]

"A quiz funnel would score these leads automatically and send personalized emails based on their answers."

[Face to camera]

"Want me to roast YOUR funnel? Free audit, link in bio."

Why it works: Pattern interrupt, demonstrates expertise, creates "I wonder what my funnel looks like" curiosity.

Testing Rules

The 72-Hour Rule: Don't touch your ad for 72 hours. Facebook needs time to learn. Constant tweaking resets the algorithm.

Week 1-2: Creative Testing

  • 1 Campaign, Messages objective
  • 1 Ad Set, Audience A (Platform Users)
  • 4 Ads (one for each creative concept)
  • Budget: $15-20/day split across ads
  • Kill any ad with CTR < 0.3% after 1,000 impressions

Week 3: Audience Testing

  • Take winning creative(s) from Week 1-2
  • Create 3 Ad Sets (Audiences A, B, C)
  • Same ads in each
  • Budget: $20-25/day split across ad sets

Week 4: Scale Winners

  • Best creative + best audience
  • Increase budget 20% every 2-3 days
  • Add retargeting campaign ($5-10/day)
  • Never more than 20% increase at once

Week-by-Week Execution Plan

Pre-Launch Checklist

  • Set up Meta Business Manager
  • Install Facebook Pixel on landing page
  • Create custom audiences (video viewers, profile visitors)
  • Build landing page for free audit
  • Set up email sequence (5 emails)
  • Record 4 ad creatives (one per concept)
  • Write ad copy (4 variations)

Week 1: Launch & Learn

Day Action
Monday Launch Campaign 1 (Messages), 4 ads, $20/day
Tue-Thu Monitor metrics (don't change anything), respond to messages within 15 min
Friday Review data, kill ads with CTR < 0.3%, identify top performers
Weekend Let ads run, prepare Week 2 variations

Week 2: Creative Optimization

Monday Duplicate winners, test different hooks (same concept, different opening)
Wednesday Mid-week check, kill underperformers, scale winners by 20%
Friday Select top 2 creatives for audience testing

Week 3: Audience Testing

Monday Create 3 ad sets (different audiences), same winning creatives
Wednesday Compare audience performance (CPL, message quality)
Friday Select winning audience, prepare for scale

Week 4: Scale & Systematize

Monday Increase budget to $35-50/day on winners, launch retargeting ($10/day)
Wednesday Review all-time performance, calculate CAC
Friday If profitable: continue scaling. If not: diagnose and iterate.
